## 👋 About Me

I'm a **final-year Data Science (Business Intelligence) student** at **Belgium Campus ITversity** in Johannesburg, South Africa. I turn raw data into clear, decision-ready insights using SQL, Python, R and Power BI.

I'm driven by curiosity and by the "so what?" behind every dataset. My work focuses on cleaning and modelling data, finding the patterns that matter, and presenting them in dashboards that business users can act on.

**Currently:** completing my degree, building out my analytics portfolio, and looking for graduate and junior opportunities in data analytics and business intelligence.
